Romania's borders, one lesson at a time

An animated history picture book for children aged 7 to 10: one big, friendly map and 14 important moments, from the Dacians to today, each a still page showing the lands of its time. Simple, roughly traced shapes, not a research atlas. The full plan is in ROADMAP.md.

  2. 01 Base geographyNatural Earth → TopoJSON, rivers, admin units
  3. 02 Satellite backdropNASA Blue Marble, reprojected once
  4. 03 The moments14 moments for children, one content file
  5. 04 A simple trace toolalign an old map, draw smooth shapes
  6. 05 The map shapesbuilt from counties, empires round them
  7. 06 The playerstill pages, icons pop in, arrows
  8. 07 The time scale and navigationa scale down the right, a triangle to drag; Back / Next, swipe and keys to come
