Romania's borders, one lesson at a time

An animated history picture book for children aged 7 to 10: one big, friendly map and about 18 important moments, from the Dacians to today, with borders that morph from one moment to the next. Simple, roughly traced shapes, not a research atlas. The full plan is in ROADMAP.md.
